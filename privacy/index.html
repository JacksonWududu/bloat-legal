Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-05

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

yanhao wu (“we,” “us”) operates BloatMonster. This policy explains how the BloatMonster app and widget handle information, and how visits to these static legal pages may be logged by the site host. Contact us at BloatMonster@163.com with privacy questions or requests. BloatMonster does not require an account.

2. Information kept on your device

The app stores the goals you create, including titles, steps, dates, completion history and reminders. It also stores your creatures, collection progress, language and other preferences, and images prepared for sharing. The app and widget use a shared storage area on your device; the widget displays the number of active goals. We do not send your goal or note text to our own server.

If you use Notes, the app stores each note’s text, color and creation time on your device. Clearing a note removes it from the visible list, but a copy of its text may remain for Undo. After the Undo period ends, the app removes that copy during its next cleanup. The app retains a local record of the note identifier, creation and clearing times, points awarded and reward status to account for rewards.

If you use Daily Check-in, the app keeps each check-in’s name, creation and update dates, and the dates and times you mark it complete in device-protected local storage that is eligible for iOS device backup. Deleting a check-in removes its name and visible history, but all recorded completion dates and times remain locally. Some of these records preserve creature points already awarded and prevent duplicate awards.

BloatMonster also keeps a local activity log for app events such as creating or completing a goal and opening the Museum. An event can include its type and time, an internal goal identifier, the goal’s creation time and elapsed days, the creature’s growth state, and the event source (such as the app icon or widget). This log is not uploaded by the app. Deleting an individual goal does not necessarily erase events already written to the local log.

3. Optional walking and Apple Health

If you turn on Daily Walking and grant permission, BloatMonster reads step counts from Apple Health to show progress toward your walking goal and award stamps and walking cards. It reads step count only, excludes samples marked as manually entered, and does not write data to Apple Health. You can withdraw Health access in your device’s Health settings. Without access, walking rewards that depend on readable steps may be unavailable.

Readable daily step totals are kept for at most seven activity days in the app’s private, device-protected cache, which is marked as excluded from device backup. Walking settings and reward records are stored separately and are eligible for iOS device backup. These records include your selected goals and enablement history, earned stamp dates and redemption records, collected walking cards and their acquisition dates, and the cumulative draw count; they contain no step totals. Turning Daily Walking off clears cached step totals and the last sync time but keeps settings and rewards. After a device restore, the app reads step totals again from Apple Health when those records are available and access is permitted. Apple Health’s own storage, backup and sync are controlled by Apple and your device settings.

In Daily Walking, “Delete walking settings and progress” removes walking settings, cached step totals and stamp records from the app. Walking cards you have already drawn remain on this device and remain eligible for device backup. A cumulative count of walking cards drawn also remains so the free draw limit cannot be reset; that count contains no step totals or dates. This action does not delete the original records in Apple Health or your separate goals and goal cards. The app does not send Apple Health step data to RevenueCat, our own server or advertising services, and does not include step totals in goal completion receipt images. We do not use Health data for advertising or marketing or sell it.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

We use RevenueCat to offer products, verify purchases and manage BloatMonster Plus access. The RevenueCat SDK creates a random anonymous app user identifier. RevenueCat may process that identifier, device and operating-system technical information, and applicable Apple purchase receipts, purchase history and subscription status. We use this information for subscription functionality, purchase validation and subscription statistics and analytics. Apple handles App Store payment; we do not receive your payment card details. Goal text, note text, and Apple Health step data are not sent to RevenueCat.

RevenueCat processes subscription information as our service provider under this policy. Its privacy policy describes its general practices. The App Store’s handling of your purchase is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. Deleting the app or asking us to delete RevenueCat data does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage or cancel it in your Apple subscription settings.

5. Notifications, sharing and device motion

If you allow notifications, BloatMonster schedules goal reminders and collection notices locally on your device. A reminder can contain your goal title and may appear on the Lock Screen according to your iOS settings. You can change notification permission in iOS settings.

When you choose to share a goal completion receipt image, the app opens the iOS share sheet. You choose the destination; the recipient service then handles the copy under its own terms and privacy policy. The app may keep a temporary or cached image to prepare that share. If you view certain collection cards, device tilt can control a visual effect. Motion readings are used in memory for that effect and are not saved or sent by the app.

6. Retention, deletion and your choices

You can delete eligible unfinished goals, clear notes, and delete walking settings and progress in the app as described above. Completed goals and collected cards cannot currently be deleted individually. Some collection records, such as Museum cards, retain goal titles and completion dates.

Local goal, note, collection, preference and activity-log data generally remains until removed through the app where available or by deleting the app. Clearing a note may leave an Undo copy and retains a local reward record, as described above. Deleting a Daily Check-in retains all recorded completion timestamps, including those used for creature points. BloatMonster does not provide its own cloud sync or backup and does not use CloudKit to sync your app data. General app data, including Daily Check-in records and walking settings and reward records, may be included in an iOS device backup, including iCloud Backup, according to your device settings. Cached step totals are marked as excluded. Recovery requires an available device backup containing the relevant data and restoration through Apple’s device setup process; simply downloading the app or restoring purchases does not recover local progress. Earlier backups cannot recover data that was excluded when they were created. Deleting data in the app does not edit existing device backups; manage those backups in Apple’s settings. Copies you have shared or saved outside BloatMonster must be managed at their destination.

To request access to or deletion of subscription information processed through RevenueCat, email BloatMonster@163.com. Because the app does not have accounts, we may need an anonymous app user identifier or purchase details sufficient to locate the record. We will handle requests in line with applicable law and retain records needed for legal or transaction purposes. Deleting a RevenueCat record does not cancel an Apple subscription. You can also withdraw optional Health and notification permissions through iOS settings.

If you contact us by email, we use your address and message to respond and handle your request, and retain the correspondence as needed for that purpose or legal obligations.

7. Static website access

When you visit these legal pages on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, including for visitors who are not signed in. GitHub may process other technical request information under its privacy statement. The pages do not ask you to create an account or submit information through a form, and we do not add advertising or analytics scripts. See GitHub’s Pages documentation for its visitor logging disclosure.

8. Security, changes and contact

We use the device storage protections described above and limit the information sent to subscription providers to what is needed for purchases and access. No storage or transmission method is perfectly secure. We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change; the last-updated date above identifies the current version. For questions or privacy requests, contact BloatMonster@163.com.

2. 儲存在裝置上的資訊

App 會儲存你建立的目標，包括標題、步驟、日期、完成紀錄及提醒，也會儲存小怪物、收藏進度、語言與其他偏好，以及準備分享的圖片。App 與小工具使用裝置上的共享儲存空間；小工具會顯示進行中的目標數量。我們不會將你的目標文字或便利貼文字傳送到自己的伺服器。

如使用「便利貼」，App 會在裝置上儲存每張便利貼的文字、顏色及建立時間。撕下便利貼後，它會從畫面上的清單移除，但文字副本可能暫時保留供「復原」使用。復原期限屆滿後，App 會在下次清理時移除該副本。App 仍會在本機保留便利貼識別碼、建立與撕下時間、獲得的怪物積分及獎勵狀態，以計算獎勵。

如使用「每日打卡」，App 會在受裝置保護、可納入 iOS 裝置備份的本機儲存空間中保留打卡名稱、建立與更新日期，以及完成打卡的日期和時間。刪除打卡會移除其名稱與可見歷史，但所有已記錄的完成日期與時間仍留在本機。其中部分紀錄用於保留已發放的怪物積分，並避免重複發放。

BloatMonster 也會在本機記錄建立或完成目標、開啟博物館等 App 事件。紀錄可能包含事件類型與時間、內部目標識別碼、目標建立時間與經過天數、怪物成長狀態，以及事件來源（例如 App 圖示或小工具）。App 不會上傳此紀錄。刪除單一目標不一定會一併刪除已寫入的本機事件紀錄。

3. 選用的步行功能與 Apple「健康」

如果你開啟「每日步行」並授予權限，BloatMonster 會從 Apple「健康」App 讀取步數，以顯示步行目標進度並發放印章與步行卡片。App 只讀取步數，排除標記為手動輸入的資料，不會將資料寫入「健康」。你可以在裝置的「健康」設定中撤銷存取權限。若未授權或撤銷權限，依賴可讀取步數的步行獎勵可能無法使用。

可讀取的每日步數總計最多保留七個活動日，儲存在 App 私有且受裝置保護的快取中，並標記為排除裝置備份。步行設定與獎勵紀錄分開儲存，可納入 iOS 裝置備份。這些紀錄包括選定的目標與啟用歷史、印章獲得日期及兌換紀錄、已收藏的步行卡片及獲得日期，以及累計抽取次數，不含步數總計。關閉「每日步行」會清除暫存步數與上次同步時間，但保留設定及獎勵。裝置還原後，App 會在資料可用且獲得存取權限時，重新從 Apple「健康」讀取步數。Apple「健康」本身的儲存、備份與同步由 Apple 及你的裝置設定管理。

在「每日步行」選擇「刪除步行設定與進度」，會從 App 移除步行設定、暫存步數總計及印章紀錄。已抽取的步行卡片仍會保留在本裝置，並可納入裝置備份。裝置上也會保留步行卡片的累計抽取次數，以防免費抽取額度被重設；這個計數不含步數總計或日期。此操作不會刪除 Apple「健康」中的原始紀錄，也不會刪除另外儲存的目標與目標卡片。App 不會將 Apple「健康」步數傳送給 RevenueCat、我們自己的伺服器或廣告服務，也不會在目標完成收據圖片中加入步數總計。我們不會將「健康」資料用於廣告或行銷，也不會出售這些資料。
