Privacy Policy — English

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

[OPERATOR_NAME] (“we,” “us”) operates BloatMonster. This policy explains how the BloatMonster app and widget handle information, and how visits to these static legal pages may be logged by the site host. Contact us at [CONTACT_EMAIL] with privacy questions or requests. BloatMonster does not require an account.

2. Information kept on your device

The app stores the tasks and goals you enter, including titles, subtasks, dates, completion history and reminders. It also stores your creatures, collection progress, language and other preferences, and images prepared for sharing. The app and widget use a shared storage area on your device so the widget can show task information. We do not send your task text to our own server.

BloatMonster also keeps a local activity log for app events such as creating or completing a task and opening the Museum. An event can include its type and time, an internal task identifier, task age or state, and the part of the app where it occurred. This log is not uploaded by the app. Deleting an individual task does not necessarily erase events already written to the local log.

3. Optional walking and Apple Health

If you turn on Daily Walking and grant permission, BloatMonster reads step counts from Apple Health to show progress toward your walking goal and award stamps and walking cards. It reads step count only, excludes samples marked as manually entered, and does not write data to Apple Health. You can withdraw Health access in your device’s Health settings. Without access, walking rewards that depend on readable steps may be unavailable.

Readable daily step totals are kept for at most seven activity days. Walking settings, earned stamps and walking cards remain on the device until you delete walking data. Turning Daily Walking off clears cached step totals and the last sync time but keeps settings and rewards. Walking data is stored in the app’s private, device-protected storage and excluded from iCloud backup. The app does not send Apple Health step data to RevenueCat, our own server or advertising services, and does not include step totals in task receipt images. We do not use Health data for advertising or marketing or sell it.

In Daily Walking, “Delete all walking data” removes walking settings, cached step totals, stamps and walking cards from the app. A lifetime count of walking cards drawn remains on this device so the free draw limit cannot be reset; that count contains no step totals or dates. This action does not delete the original records in Apple Health or your separate tasks and task collection.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

We use RevenueCat to offer products, verify purchases and manage BloatMonster Plus access. The RevenueCat SDK creates a random anonymous app user identifier. RevenueCat may process that identifier, device and operating-system technical information, and applicable Apple purchase receipts, purchase history and subscription status. We use this information for subscription functionality, purchase validation and subscription reporting. Apple handles App Store payment; we do not receive your payment card details. Task text and Apple Health step data are not sent to RevenueCat.

RevenueCat processes subscription information as our service provider. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy for its practices. The App Store’s handling of your purchase is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. Deleting the app or asking us to delete RevenueCat data does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage or cancel it in your Apple subscription settings.

5. Notifications, sharing and device motion

If you allow notifications, BloatMonster schedules task reminders and collection notices locally on your device. A reminder can contain your task title and may appear on the Lock Screen according to your iOS settings. You can change notification permission in iOS settings.

When you choose to share a task receipt image, the app opens the iOS share sheet. You choose the destination; the recipient service then handles the copy under its own terms and privacy policy. The app may keep a temporary or cached image to prepare that share. If you view certain collection cards, device tilt can control a visual effect. Motion readings are used in memory for that effect and are not saved or sent by the app.

6. Retention, deletion and your choices

You can delete individual tasks in the app and delete walking data from Daily Walking as described above. Local task, collection, preference and activity-log data generally remains until removed through the app where available or by deleting the app. The app does not provide its own cloud sync or backup. Ordinary app data may be included in an iOS device backup according to your device settings; the private walking storage is excluded from iCloud backup. Copies you have shared or saved outside BloatMonster must be managed at their destination.

To request access to or deletion of subscription information processed through RevenueCat, email [CONTACT_EMAIL]. Because the app does not have accounts, we may need an anonymous app user identifier or purchase details sufficient to locate the record. We will handle requests in line with applicable law and the records needed for legal or transaction purposes. Deleting a RevenueCat record does not cancel an Apple subscription. You can also withdraw optional Health and notification permissions through iOS settings.

If you contact us by email, we use your address and message to respond and handle your request, and retain the correspondence as needed for that purpose or legal obligations.

7. Static website access

These legal pages are intended for GitHub Pages. When published there, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, including for visitors who are not signed in. GitHub may process other technical request information under its privacy statement. The pages do not ask you to create an account or submit information through a form, and we do not add advertising or analytics scripts. See GitHub’s Pages documentation for its visitor logging disclosure.

8. Security, changes and contact

We use the device storage protections described above and limit the information sent to subscription providers to what is needed for purchases and access. No storage or transmission method is perfectly secure. We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change; the effective date above identifies the current version. For questions or privacy requests, contact [CONTACT_EMAIL].

2. 儲存在裝置上的資訊

App 會儲存你輸入的任務與目標，包括標題、子任務、日期、完成紀錄及提醒，也會儲存小怪物、收藏進度、語言與其他偏好，以及準備分享的圖片。App 與小工具使用裝置上的共享儲存空間，讓小工具能顯示任務資訊。我們不會將你的任務文字傳送到自己的伺服器。

BloatMonster 也會在本機記錄建立或完成任務、開啟博物館等 App 事件。紀錄可能包含事件類型與時間、內部任務識別碼、任務歷時或狀態，以及事件發生的 App 位置。App 不會上傳此紀錄。刪除單一任務不一定會一併刪除已寫入的本機事件紀錄。

3. 選用的步行功能與 Apple「健康」

如果你開啟「每日步行」並授予權限，BloatMonster 會從 Apple「健康」讀取步數，以顯示步行目標進度並發放印章與步行卡片。App 只讀取步數，排除標記為手動輸入的資料，不會將資料寫入「健康」。你可以在裝置的「健康」設定中撤銷存取權限。撤銷後，依賴可讀取步數的步行獎勵可能無法使用。

可讀取的每日步數總計最多保留七個活動日。步行設定、已獲得的印章和步行卡片會留在裝置上，直到你刪除步行資料。關閉「每日步行」會清除暫存的步數總計與上次同步時間，但保留設定及獎勵。步行資料儲存在 App 私有且受裝置保護的空間，並排除於 iCloud 備份之外。App 不會將 Apple「健康」步數傳送給 RevenueCat、我們自己的伺服器或廣告服務，也不會在任務收據圖片中加入步數總計。我們不會將「健康」資料用於廣告或行銷，也不會出售這些資料。

在「每日步行」選擇「刪除所有步行資料」，會從 App 移除步行設定、暫存步數總計、印章及步行卡片。裝置上仍會保留已抽取步行卡片的終身次數，以防免費抽取額度被重設；這個計數不含步數總計或日期。此操作不會刪除 Apple「健康」中的原始紀錄，也不會刪除另外儲存的任務與任務收藏。
